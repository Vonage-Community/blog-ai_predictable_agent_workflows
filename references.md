# References

This document contains additional resources related to agentic systems, ReAct loops, and reproducible analytical workflows. These materials informed the concepts explored in the accompanying Vonage article.

## Research Papers

### ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models

Yao, Shunyu, et al. (2023)

Introduces the ReAct framework, which combines reasoning and action in language model agents through iterative loops of thought and tool use.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629

---

### Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback

Bai, Yuntao, et al. (2022)

Explores mechanisms for guiding and constraining model behavior through explicit principles and structured feedback.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073

---

## About This Repository

The examples in this repository are intended to demonstrate workflow design patterns that can improve consistency in agentic systems. They are not production-ready implementations and have been simplified to focus on the concepts discussed in the accompanying article.
